lighthouse-v3.png

Derived from "Lighthouse.svg" by matthewg42 (2008), released into the public
domain by the author. Source:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Lighthouse.svg

Recoloured to the scene's night palette and downscaled to 120x480 (4x the
rendered size, so it stays sharp on retina).

The lantern room -- rows 0-74: finial, roof, glazing, gallery deck, railing --
is erased to transparent. At render size that housing is about 20x17 px and
reads as a bright rectangular window frame around the light, which is what made
the lamp look square. The lamp is drawn as disc geometry in water.js instead,
and LAMP_V=75/480 puts it at the top of the remaining shaft.

The filename carries a version because the browser's image cache keys on URL
and holds a decoded copy that survives reloads and server restarts; editing the
art in place shows the old sprite indefinitely.
