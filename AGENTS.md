# Portfolio

A portfolio where someone deciding whether to work with Alex can see what each tool does
in a few seconds, and go to the code.

## Principles

General rules for look and feel, each starting with the time and the site's version
(`VERSION`) when it was made, with the reason given when it came up. A ruling on one
feature goes in its file in `features/`, under Calls.

- 2026-10-15 22:55, 1.0.0. Motion goes only where attention already is, one motion at a time. A
  moving picture pulls attention off whatever the reader is looking at, so the text
  beside it gets hard to read.
- 2026-10-15 22:55, 1.0.0. A still shows the thing at its best moment, not its first, and leaving a
  state doesn't jump. A reader judges from the still; the user found Vault's channel page
  "way better" than its empty first screen.
- 2026-10-16 00:03, 1.0.0. Nothing moves or covers what the user is aiming at or using. A card that
  rose on hover made it feel like the pointer would miss the icon on it, and the
  browser's video controls hid the recorded app's own.
- 2026-10-16 21:20, 1.0.0. Each thing has one link, marked where it is, and everything else can be
  selected like text. The mark is what says where the link is: a separate label for it
  was tried and dropped.
- 2026-10-16 00:44, 1.0.0. Where the platform already does something better, hand it over. Our large
  view on a phone was barely bigger than the card; the system's full screen can turn to
  landscape.
- 2026-10-16 23:18, 1.0.0. A demo frames the real thing. It may stage the input (a drawn pointer,
  typed text, a scripted prompt), since those are obviously limits of recording, but it
  doesn't show what the app can't do or draw anything larger than it is. A timer
  enlarged to match a zoomed page made WebTime's nudge look enormous.
- 2026-10-17 11:25, 1.0.0. Whatever isn't the subject is removed or made to blend in, explanations
  included: one stays only as long as it's needed. Anything that stands out draws
  attention it hasn't earned; a light, bare fake site stood out in a dark clip.
